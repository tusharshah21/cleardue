### Deviations from the brief
- `partial_payment` reissues one replacement invoice (same items, first instalment as the minimum) instead of editing the sent original. It reuses the split pipeline and its safety checks, and it avoids updating a sent invoice in place. The demo freelancer is in India, so the demo path is `split_invoices`.
- The client can accept a plan without a second approval only when the freelancer already approved that offer, or when autopilot is on. With autopilot off and no offer, plans aren't shown, and the client can ask in the chat, which escalates.
- The money-figure guard in `payerAssistant` is deterministic: any amount or percentage that doesn't appear in the sources forces an escalation. It can over-escalate (for example an answer that computes a correct sum not written anywhere), which I think is the right trade-off for a page that talks about money.
- The pay page's "Pay with PayPal" button opens PayPal's hosted invoice page. There's no in-page PayPal checkout, since the payment belongs to the PayPal invoice.
